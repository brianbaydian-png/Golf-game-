# Pinecrest Links

A 9-hole 3D golf game (three.js) in a single HTML file, styled after mobile swipe-golf games. Open `index.html` in a browser to play: main menu, course card and loading screen lead into the round.

- Swipe on the course: drag down from the ball for the backswing (a meter beside your finger shows how far back you are, up to 110%), then swipe back up
- Shot shaping: the line you pull down sets the face, your upswipe is the path. Up the same line = straight; up to the right of it = draw (starts right, curves left); up to the left = fade; further off = hook / slice
- After each strike: swing speed, backswing and downswing angles, and swing offset (green when inside the club's allowance)
- Golf Dreams bag (P, D 295, 3w 255, 3i–9i 225–165, Pw 130, Sw 110, Lw 100), lies that cost power (Fairway ~95%, Rough ~82%), golf balls with speed / spin / forgiveness
- Club sheet (blue button), shot type (orange button: Regular, Punch, Chip, Flop), binoculars to look at the target
- Lie, distance and wind tags; hole card with score and shot number
- Wind, fairway/rough/bunker lies, water, palms, out of bounds, green tracer with carry readout
- Raised, tilted greens you can see, with a red grid and drifting dots; a real cup the ball drops into, with lip-outs when it is hit too hard or catches the edge
- Settings: sound, course map, scorecard, how to play
