Pinecrest Links

  • Swing: put your finger on the ball, drag down to take the club back (further = harder), then swipe back up through the ball.
  • Straight shots: swipe back up along the same line you pulled down. Coming up to the right of that line fades or slices the ball; to the left draws or hooks it.
  • Aim: drag sideways on the course. The binoculars show where you are aiming.
  • Clubs: tap the blue button. The orange button picks Regular, Punch, Chip or Flop.
  • Putting: the flag comes out, and the red grid and drifting dots show the slope.
