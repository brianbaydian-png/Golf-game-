Birdie

Swipe right on the course. Drag down to take the club back: the further you pull, the harder you hit, and pulling very far is an overswing that is harder to control. Then swipe up through the ball. Drift right on the way up and the ball fades or slices; drift left and it draws or hooks. A slow upswipe loses power. Drag sideways to aim, or tap a spot on the map. Change clubs with the arrows (your caddie picks one for you). Keyboard: ←→ aim, ↑↓ club.
