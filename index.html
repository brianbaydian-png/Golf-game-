Pinecrest Links

  • Swing: drag down anywhere on the course to take the club back (further = harder, up to full power), then flick up. The club swings through for you.
  • Shape it: pull straight down for a straight shot. For a fade, pull back clearly to the left; for a draw, clearly to the right. A slight tilt barely bends it; a big angle bends it a lot. Then swipe up through the white fan above the ball. Swinging through the fan strikes it out of the middle; missing it hits the toe or heel, which costs distance and bends the ball more.
  • Aim: drag sideways on the course. The binoculars show where you are aiming.
  • Clubs: tap the blue button. The orange button picks Regular, Punch, Chip or Flop.
  • My Bag: on the main menu (or in Settings), pick a model for every club. Each one looks different and has its own distance, loft, curl (how much it shapes), spin, forgiveness and recovery from rough and sand.
  • Putting: the greens are quick and full of humps and hollows. The white line shows how your putt will start to break. As you pull back, a ring shows where it will stop, so pull until the ring sits just past the hole. The red grid and drifting dots show the slope.
